Ancient History

6000 BCE - 650 CE

Ancient history is the time period in history between the origins of human civilization and the fall of ancient empires.

Medieval Period

475 CE - 1500 CE

Middle Ages, Period in European history traditionally dated from the fall of the Roman Empire to the dawn of the Renaissance. In the 5th century the Western Roman Empire endured declines in population, economic vitality, and the size and prominence of cities.

The Renaissance

14th century - 17th century

The Renaissance was the time period in history that saw increased European explorations, the invention of the printing press, and the development of international trade.

Age of Revolution

1750 CE - 1945

The Age of Revolution, also referred to as the Late Modern Period, encompasses a transformative era in human history spanning roughly from the late 18th to the early 20th century.

Modern Era

1945 - present

Modern Era, also known as the Contemporary Period or Present Era, encompasses the period from the late 20th century to the present day, representing a dynamic and rapidly evolving phase of human history.
